Since 2021, OBI has grown from a small Sudanese beauty brand into a collection of everyday beauty and self-care essentials.

EST. 2021

WHERE IT STARTED

From Obi Corner to OBI.

OBI Cosmetics began in Sudan in 2021 with a simple vision: to create products that bring confidence, beauty and joy into everyday life.

Originally known as Obi Corner, the journey started with hair care essentials before expanding into makeup.

The introduction of our signature lip glosses became a defining chapter in the OBI story, opening the door to new products, new ideas and a wider vision for the brand.
