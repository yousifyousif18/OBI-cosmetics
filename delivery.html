DELIVERY QUESTIONS

Good to know.

How long does delivery take? +

Cairo and Giza orders are normally delivered within up to 3 days. Other Egyptian governorates, the UAE and Qatar may take up to 4 days. Delivery times in Saudi Arabia depend on the selected provider, RedBox or SMSA, and the delivery location.

Which delivery options are available in Saudi Arabia? +

Saudi Arabia orders can currently be delivered through RedBox or SMSA. The available option and delivery fee are selected during checkout. RedBox is 20 SAR and SMSA is 35 SAR.

Can I get my order the same day? +

Same-day delivery can be arranged in Egypt through Uber where available. The order must be paid in advance before dispatch.

How do I pay for same-day delivery? +

Same-day orders can be paid in advance through Vodafone Cash or Bank of Khartoum.

What if I entered the wrong address? +

Contact OBI as soon as possible. Once an order has been dispatched, changing the delivery address may not be possible.
